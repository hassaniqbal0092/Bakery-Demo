Why Ember & Oak

A hearth bakery, not a display case

Actually wood-fired

Our hearth oven is fed with local hardwood, giving every loaf a deep, blistered crust you won't get from a standard deck oven.

Long fermentation

Our sourdough starter has been alive since 2018. Doughs rest up to 60 hours for flavor and easier digestion.

Cakes built to order

No walk-in cake case — every celebration cake is designed one at a time with our pastry chef, from flavor to filling to message.
