Our Story

From a countertop starter to a wood-fired hearth

Ember & Oak began in 2018 with one home oven, one sourdough starter, and a lot of curious neighbors. Today we're a full hearth bakery in Asheville's River Arts District — but the process hasn't changed much.

Founder Maren Kessler started baking sourdough after a stint working bread shifts in Lyon, France. Back in Asheville, she kept a starter alive on her kitchen counter and began selling loaves at the local farmers market out of a cooler bag — three varieties, twenty loaves a week, gone within the hour.

By 2018 the market table had turned into a lease on Depot Street, and a proper wood-fired hearth oven followed a year later. That oven is still the heart of the bakery: every loaf, whether it's our everyday country sourdough or a holiday centerpiece, passes through it.

In 2020, pastry chef Diego Solis joined to build out our viennoiserie program and — because customers kept asking — our custom cake studio. What makes Ember & Oak different isn't a secret recipe; it's that we still bake in small batches, ferment doughs for days instead of hours, and build every custom cake from scratch with the person who ordered it.
